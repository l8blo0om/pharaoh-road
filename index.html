Just 4 miles from downtown Columbia, Pharoah Road sits in Burton Heights — a quiet residential area with easy access to everything that matters. Travel nurses working Richland Memorial Hospital (1.5 miles), Prisma Health Baptist, or Columbia Regional Care Center choose this location for its commute.
