The 45-second script

Same opener for every studio call. Just plug in the format and location.

"Hi, I'm Dr. Nicole Bishop — I'm a certified Pilates instructor launching a small-group postnatal Pilates class for mothers with infants, mat-based only, no reformers. I'm looking to rent weekday morning studio time — usually a 9 to 11 slot, two mornings a week. Do you rent to outside instructors, and if so, could you tell me your rate and your policy on infants on premises? I have my own commercial liability policy ready to send a COI from."

That answer sorts them into yes-let's-talk, no-we-don't-rent, or maybe-let-me-check in under 90 seconds. Log which one in Notes, cycle the status pill, move to the next number.

Don't waste time on these (why)
  • Any Club Pilates location — franchise programming, won't rent to independents
  • JETSET, IM=X, BODYBAR, Studio Fire, Level Fitness, The Pilates Studio Irmo — reformer-focused, wrong format + moving equipment near infants
  • HOTWORX Fort Mill — heated only, not safe for babies
  • Onelife Fitness (any location) — big-box, staff-only class schedule
  • Orangetheory, F45, Anytime Fitness — same
  • Martial arts dojos — weapons on walls, heavy bags, competition trophies at kid height
  • Carolina Fitness Rentals (Fort Mill) — equipment rental, not studio space
